# Brag Plan: Tanker Tracker

## What is this app?
A real-time geopolitical intelligence dashboard that tracks Middle East oil tankers on a live map — fusing AIS positions, oil prices, sanctions data, news, and dark-fleet anomaly detection into one Bloomberg-terminal-style command center.

## The angle
Not a SaaS demo — an **intelligence terminal**. The video should feel like a classified ops console booting up: black screen, amber monospace, the Gulf lighting up with vessel contacts, then a single sanctioned tanker getting "made" — identity, risk score, the works. The flex is that a personal project looks and behaves like a Bloomberg/Palantir-grade situational display, and every data layer is now keyless/open-source.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Black. A single amber cursor blip. Then `TANKER TRACKER` types in monospace as the Strait of Hormuz map resolves underneath with dozens of vessel dots igniting across the Gulf. One word of authority: **"LIVE."**

## Key moments (the middle)
- The map: 140+ vessel contacts across the Persian Gulf / Gulf of Oman, color-coded by threat state; chokepoint boxes (Hormuz / Bab el-Mandeb / Suez) glowing amber.
- The catch: click a vessel → **VESSEL DETAIL** dossier slides in — SILVER TRIUMPH, IMO, flag, and a **RISK SCORE** with factor bars (Going Dark, Sanctions, Flag Risk, Loitering, STS).
- The stat punch: three cards snapping in — `140 VESSELS TRACKED` / `16 SANCTIONED` / `24 ACTIVE ANOMALIES`.
- The flex line: **"Zero paid API keys."** — map, prices, news, sanctions all keyless/open-source.

## Outro / punchline
Cut to black terminal line. Amber type: **"TANKER TRACKER — the oil war, in real time."** cursor blinks.

## User flow worth showing
entry (map of the Gulf, contacts live) → key action (click a suspicious vessel) → result (full risk dossier with sanctions + dark-fleet score). This is the centerpiece — the product *doing* intelligence work, not describing it.

## Tone
- Preset: cinematic
- Creative direction: "declassified maritime-intelligence terminal booting up"
- Interpretation: big type, dramatic but restrained reveals, dark negative space, confident holds. Tension, not chaos. Motion is purposeful — contacts igniting, a dossier locking on — never frantic.

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080
## Duration: 20 seconds

## Visual identity (from the project)
- Background: true black `#000000`
- Accent: amber `#f59e0b`
- Text: white `#ffffff` / gray `#6b7280` for secondary
- Display font: JetBrains Mono (the app's only font — monospace throughout)
- Body font: JetBrains Mono
- Strongest visual element: the dark CARTO map of the Gulf with amber chokepoint boxes and color-coded vessel dots; the VESSEL DETAIL risk-score dossier panel.

## Share copy (draft)
Built a real-time oil-tanker intelligence terminal — live AIS, sanctions, dark-fleet risk scoring, anomaly detection. Bloomberg-terminal vibes, zero paid API keys. 🛢️📡

## Audio direction
- Role: cinematic support — low tension bed with a rising swell into the dossier lock-on.
- Music: cinematic/tension bed (Hyperframes selects from bundled tracks).
- Music treatment: quiet start under the hook, swell into the vessel-detail reveal, fade under the final terminal line.
- Music cue guidance: detect cues at composition time; target one strong cue for the dossier lock-on (~10-12s) and one for the stat cards; beat-grid the 3 stat cards.
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle — let the map/chokepoint glow and dossier panel presence breathe with the music RMS. No waveform/EQ bars.
- SFX posture: sparse, motion-matched — a soft radar-blip on contacts igniting, a locking "acquire" cue on the dossier, three ticks on the stat cards.
- Audio-coupled moments: title typing, contacts igniting, dossier lock-on, 3 stat cards, final line.
- Restraint rule: no chaotic layering, no strobing; this is an ops console, not a game ad.

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — Boot / hook — 3s
Black screen. Amber cursor blip center. `TANKER TRACKER` types in monospace. The Gulf map fades up beneath it; a scatter of vessel dots ignites across the Persian Gulf. Big amber word: **LIVE**.
Sequential/interaction: yes — vessel dots ignite in a quick staggered burst.
Audio intent: quiet tension, a soft radar blip as dots appear.
Audio-coupled idea: title types with subtle key ticks; blips on dot ignition.
Music: low tension bed entering.
Transition mood: dramatic → Scene 2

### Scene 2 — The board — 4s
Full dashboard map of the Gulf: 140+ color-coded contacts, amber chokepoint boxes labeled STRAIT OF HORMUZ / BAB EL-MANDEB / SUEZ. Small overlay label: **"All vessels. Middle East. Near real-time."**
Sequential/interaction: none (the board is already alive); subtle chokepoint glow.
Audio intent: bed builds slightly; room tone of a live system.
Audio-coupled idea: subtle audio-reactive glow on chokepoint boxes.
Music: tension bed rising.
Transition mood: clean → Scene 3

### Scene 3 — Lock-on (the flow centerpiece) — 5s
A cursor moves to a suspicious dot near Hormuz and clicks. The **VESSEL DETAIL** dossier slides in from the right: SILVER TRIUMPH, IMO 9916376, flag, Tanker (82), and a **RISK SCORE** with factor bars (Going Dark, Sanctions, Flag Risk, Loitering, STS). This is the money shot.
Sequential/interaction: yes — simulate the cursor click; dossier slides in; risk bars fill left-to-right.
Audio intent: rising swell resolves into a "lock/acquire" hit on the dossier appearing.
Audio-coupled idea: acquire cue on panel slide-in; soft ticks as risk bars fill.
Music: swell → strong cue on lock-on (beat-locked).
Transition mood: dramatic → Scene 4

### Scene 4 — The stats — 4s
Three cards snap in one by one on the dark field: **140 VESSELS TRACKED**, **16 SANCTIONED**, **24 ACTIVE ANOMALIES** — amber numerals counting up fast.
Sequential/interaction: yes — 3 cards arrive one by one, numbers count up.
Audio intent: three clean ticks, one per card, on the beat grid.
Audio-coupled idea: beat-grid the 3 cards; count-up ticks.
Music: bed steady, percussive hits align to cards.
Transition mood: hard → Scene 5

### Scene 5 — The flex + outro — 4s
Cut to near-black. Line 1 (amber): **"Zero paid API keys."** small subline: live AIS · FRED prices · OpenSanctions · keyless map. Beat. Then the outro line: **"TANKER TRACKER — the oil war, in real time."** cursor blinks.
Sequential/interaction: none; two held lines.
Audio intent: music fades under the final line; one last soft blip on the blinking cursor.
Audio-coupled idea: final cursor blink blip; music fade-out.
Music: fade to silence under outro.
Transition mood: slow crossfade → end
